Paul Assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant integration for Google Workspace.

What this app does

Paul Assistant is a personal automation tool, operated by a single individual for their own Google account. It connects to Google APIs to help the account owner organise their own information. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

  • Gmail — prepares daily summaries of the owner's own inbox and, on request, drafts or sends messages.
  • Google Calendar — reads the owner's calendars and creates, moves or deletes the owner's own events.
  • Google Drive & Docs — reads the owner's own files and saves generated documents.
  • Tasks, Contacts, Sheets, Slides, Forms — reads and updates the owner's own items.

All processing runs on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is served to third parties.
