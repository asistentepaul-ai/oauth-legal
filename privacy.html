Privacy Policy

Paul Assistant · Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Paul Assistant ("the App") handles information accessed through Google APIs. The App is a private, single-user tool used by one individual ("the Owner") to organise their own Google account. It has no other users.

1. Who we are

The App is operated privately by an individual. Contact: asistentepaul@gmail.com.

2. What data the App accesses

After the Owner grants consent, the App may access the following data belonging to the Owner's own Google account:

  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.send, gmail.compose, gmail.modify, gmail.labels, gmail.settings.basic) — to read, summarise, draft, send and label the Owner's own email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar, calendar.events, calendar.readonly) — to read, create, modify and delete the Owner's own events.
  • Google Drive (drive, drive.file, drive.readonly) — to read the Owner's own files and save documents created for the Owner.
  • Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms — to read and generate the Owner's own documents.
  • Tasks and Contacts — to read and update the Owner's own tasks and contacts.
  • Basic profile (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile) — to identify which account is connected.

The App does not access data belonging to any other person and offers no mechanism for third parties to connect their accounts.

3. How data is stored

OAuth refresh tokens and derived data are stored locally on the Owner's personal computer, protected by the operating system's file permissions. No Google user data is stored on external servers, and no database of Google user content is maintained.

4. How data is used

Data is used solely to perform tasks the Owner explicitly requests, such as producing a summary of the Owner's inbox, creating a calendar event, or saving a document. Data is processed transiently by a language model in order to generate those responses.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or transfer Google user data to any third party. Data is disclosed only if strictly required by applicable law, or with the Owner's explicit instruction to send a message or share a file.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Paul Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features described above.
  • Data is not transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
  • Data is not used for advertising and is not sold.
  • No humans read the data, except where the Owner themself is the reader, or for security, legal or abuse purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as needed for the features above. The Owner may revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting the locally stored token files. Revoking access stops all further data collection immediately; locally cached copies can be deleted from disk at the Owner's discretion.

8. Security

Authentication uses Google OAuth 2.0, with tokens stored only on the Owner's own machine. The App runs locally and is not publicly reachable.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children and is not available to the public.

10. Changes

Any material change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: asistentepaul@gmail.com.
